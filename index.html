Who We Are

A Contractor Built on Compliance, Craft and Clear Communication

Jayfel Engineering Limited is a construction and civil engineering company registered with the National Construction Authority (NCA) and based in Kilifi, Kenya. We work as main contractor on apartment developments and private residential blocks, handling every stage — mobilisation, site clearance, surveying, earthworks, concrete works and finishes.

  • NCA Certificate of Compliance on completed developments
  • Valid KRA tax compliance certification
