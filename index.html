Who We Are

Your Housing Partner

Jayfel Engineering Limited is a Kenyan construction and civil engineering firm based in Kilifi, registered with the National Construction Authority. We design, build and manage residential developments — from multi-storey apartment blocks to private villas — along the Kenyan Coast.

Our mission is simple: to elevate the housing standards of humanity, one well-built home at a time.
