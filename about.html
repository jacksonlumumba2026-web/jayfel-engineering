Our Story

Built in Kilifi, Trusted Along the Coast

Jayfel Engineering Limited is a construction and civil engineering company registered with the National Construction Authority (NCA) and based in Robert Muli Gardens, Kilifi District, Kenya.

We work directly with developers and private clients to deliver residential apartment blocks, maisonettes and villas — managing every stage of a build from groundbreaking and foundation work through to roofing, finishing and handover.

Our project record spans multi-unit developments such as Mtwapa Pride II and III, private residential villas including Marijani Villa and Velma Flat, and custom homes built for individual clients across Kilifi and Kwale counties.
