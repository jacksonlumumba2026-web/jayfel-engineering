Jayfel Engineering Limited is a Kenyan private limited company delivering building and civil works with full National Construction Authority registration.

Our History

From Incorporation to Certified Delivery

Jayfel Engineering Limited was incorporated under the Companies Act, 2015 on 5th September 2019 as a private limited company (No. PVT-JZUZPJB).

In July 2022 the company was registered by the National Construction Authority as a Building Works contractor (Category NCA5) and a Road Works contractor (Category NCA8), with a contractor annual practising licence issued alongside.

Our flagship delivery, Mtwapa Pride II in Kilifi, was certified by the NCA as fully compliant and was officially opened on 8th October 2022. Work continues on Mtwapa Pride III and further private residential developments.
