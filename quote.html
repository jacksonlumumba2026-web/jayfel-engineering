Common Questions

Before You Submit

How long does it take to get a quote back?

We review most requests within a few working days. Larger or more complex projects may need a site visit before we can give you an accurate estimate.

Do I need architectural drawings already?

No — if you don't have drawings yet, tell us what you're picturing and we can guide you through the design and approval process as part of the project.

Do you work outside Kilifi and Kwale?

Our project history is concentrated along the Coast, but get in touch with your location and we'll let you know if we can take it on.

Is requesting a quote free?

Yes — there's no cost or obligation to request a quote from us.
